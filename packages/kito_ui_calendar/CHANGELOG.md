## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoMonthCalendar` (single, multiple and range selection with gliding
  selection circles and a continuous range band, events dots, heatmap, min/max, past and
  unavailable days, Today capsule, rolling title), `KitoWeekStrip`, `KitoYearOverview` with a
  zoom into the month, `KitoTimeSlotPicker` with `KitoCalendarSlotSchedule`, `KitoDayTimeline`
  with overlap layout and a live now line, `KitoAgendaList` with sticky day headers,
  `KitoDateRangeBar` with a preset sheet, `KitoCalendarScope` / `KitoCalendarConfig` (locale,
  first weekday and weekend from `intl`, clock, 24-hour time, range formatting), and the pure
  `KitoCalendarMath`, `KitoCalendarMonthGrid`, `KitoCalendarEventLayout`, range and preset
  models. Right-to-left, Reduce Motion and screen readers supported throughout.
