## 0.1.0

- First release: semantic haptics (`success`, `warning`, `error`, `selection`, `impact`) with a
  global switch; `KitoHapticPattern` and `KitoHapticEvent` with nine presets, repeat and scale;
  a timed pattern player built on Flutter's `HapticFeedback`; `KitoHapticOnChange`; and
  `KitoHapticVisualizer` (bars and waveform, with a playhead). Screen-reader strings in English,
  Swahili and French.
