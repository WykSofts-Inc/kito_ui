## 0.1.0

- First release: fixed and locale-aware money (`KitoCurrency` with KES and seven more, compact,
  signed, cents on demand), numbers (compact, grouped, percent, signed percent, ordinals), dates
  (relative, abbreviated, day labels, time ranges, greetings), durations, file sizes, distances,
  Kenyan phone numbers (parsing, printing, masking, carrier, as-you-type and a
  `TextInputFormatter`), and the `KitoFormattedNumberText`, `KitoFormattedCountingText` and
  `KitoFormattedChangeBadge` widgets. English, Swahili and French words.
