## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoCarousel` with seven effects (none, scale, rotate, parallax, cover flow,
  fade, stack), peek, looping, spring snapping and auto-play that pauses for touch and screen
  readers, driven by `KitoCarouselController`; `KitoCarouselPageIndicator` (dots, capsule,
  liquid worm, numbers, progress) with tap and scrub; `KitoBannerCarousel`; `KitoSwipeDeck` with
  tilt, stamps, momentum throws, undo, controls and `KitoSwipeDeckController`;
  `KitoStackedCards`; `KitoStoryRing`, `KitoStoryTray` with `KitoYourStory`, and
  `KitoStoryViewer` with segmented bars, tap zones, hold to pause, a 3D cube between people,
  swipe to dismiss, replies and a heart burst; `KitoParallaxHeader` with a pinned strip;
  `KitoPagedList`; `KitoSnapGrid`; `KitoInfiniteMarquee`; and the pure
  `KitoCarouselLoopMath`, `KitoCarouselAutoPlayClock`, `KitoCarouselWormMath`,
  `KitoSwipeDeckDecision` and `KitoStoryPlayback`. Right-to-left, Reduce Motion and screen
  readers supported throughout.
