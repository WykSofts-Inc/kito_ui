## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoTabBar` in nine styles (classic, floating, pill, underline, bubble with a
  gliding notch, glass, segmented, minimal, notched with a centre action), `KitoTabBarController`
  with mutable items, badges and re-tap reporting, `KitoTabScaffold`, `KitoTabBarShape`,
  `KitoSideMenu` with six transitions (push, overlay, reveal, scale, 3D, floating), drawer
  building blocks and `KitoSideRail`, `KitoTopTabs` (underline, pill, chips) and a typed
  `KitoRouter` / `KitoRouterView`.
