## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoMap` on flutter_map with OpenStreetMap tiles (`KitoMapTiles`, dark-mode
  tiles, custom servers, `KitoMapTiles.none()` for tests and offline), `KitoMapController`
  (eased glides, zoom, fit points; jumps under Reduce Motion), `KitoMapPin` in six styles
  (dot, icon, price bubble, avatar, teardrop, pulsing live dot with heading) drawn by
  `KitoMapPinView`, `KitoMapClusterer` and `KitoMapClusterView`, `KitoMapRoute` with casing,
  dashes, start and end caps and travelled progress, a user-location puck with accuracy
  circle (`KitoMapPulse`), `KitoMapControls`, `KitoMapPlaceCard` for the selected pin, and pure
  `KitoMapGeometry` and `KitoMapFormat`. Right-to-left and screen readers supported.
