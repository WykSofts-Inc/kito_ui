## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoToastCenter` (single or stacked presentation, queue, pausable timers, update, progress, complete, promise, undo with countdown), `KitoToastHost` (its own overlay layer above every route and dialog, pass-through touches, swipe to dismiss, tap to fan out a stack, hover pauses), card, pill, banner, glass and island layouts, avatars, actions, haptics, `KitoToastAppearance`, and the reusable `KitoToastView`, `KitoToastCountdownRing` and `KitoToastProgressBar`.
