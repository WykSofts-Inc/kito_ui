## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoChatView` (grouped bubbles, day sections with a floating day pill, an
  unread divider it opens at, read receipts, reactions with a long-press lift overlay, swipe to
  reply, tap for the time, quote jumps, a counting scroll-to-latest button, animated inserts and
  deletes, full-screen photos), `KitoChatController`, four `KitoChatBubbleStyle`s (modern,
  minimal, glass, iMessage) with tails and `KitoChatWallpaper`s (plain, aurora, dots),
  `KitoChatComposer` with a growing field, mic-to-send morph and hold-to-record (slide to cancel,
  slide up to lock), voice notes with a scrubbable waveform and 1× / 1.5× / 2× playback,
  `KitoChatVoiceRecorder` and `KitoChatAudioPlayer` adapters with simulated fallbacks,
  `KitoChatHeader`, `KitoChatTypingIndicator`, `KitoChatStatusTicks`, `KitoChatAvatar`, inline
  markdown and link detection (`KitoChatMarkdown`), `KitoChatList` inbox with pinned rows and
  swipe actions (`KitoChatSwipeActions`), the pure `KitoChatTimeline` and `KitoChatDateFormat`,
  and bundled English, Swahili and French strings with a `KitoChatStrings.provider` override.
  Right-to-left, Reduce Motion and screen readers supported throughout.
