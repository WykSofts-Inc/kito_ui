## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoLineChart` (linear, smooth, Catmull–Rom and stepped lines, markers,
  solid and gradient areas, stroke gradients, glow, dashes, reference lines, axes, several
  series, scrubbing with a callout, morphing data), `KitoSparkline` with trend colours,
  `KitoBarChart` (grouped, stacked, vertical and horizontal, tap to highlight),
  `KitoPieChart` (pie and donut, centre content, gaps, pull-out selection), faux-3D
  `KitoBar3DChart` and `KitoPie3DChart`, `KitoChartLegend`, `KitoChartTheme` /
  `KitoChartThemeScope`, and the `KitoChartScale`, `KitoChartTicks`, `KitoChartFormat` and
  `KitoChartMath` helpers. Right-to-left, Reduce Motion and screen readers supported
  throughout.
