## 0.1.0

- First release: `KitoButton` (six variants, three sizes plus custom, icons, content slots, async
  loading → success / failure phases with icon morphing and shake), `KitoButtonController`,
  `KitoAddToCartButton` with seven choreographies, fly-to-cart flights (`KitoFlightController`,
  `KitoFlightLayer`, `KitoFlightAnchor`), `KitoBadgeButton`, `KitoButtonTheme` and
  `KitoButtonStyles.material` for plain Material buttons. English, Swahili and French strings.
